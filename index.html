How we hire

How the process works

Each step has a purpose.

  1. Application

    Submit through Northstarz from the role page. We use AI as part of screening; keep materials accurate.

  2. Work sample

    A scoped problem close to the job. We pay for take-homes that exceed two focused hours.

  3. Deep dive

    Live session with the people you would work with.

  4. Collaboration

    How you give and receive disagreement, and how you treat confidential information.

  5. Offer

    Comp, equity, start date, manager, and 30-60-90 in writing.

FAQ

Direct answers

Do I need a PhD or prior foundation-model experience?

No. Systems, data, and platform roles do not require it. Prior foundation-model experience helps for research scientist roles. Work you can point to matters more than the credential.

Is this TensorStudio or Soket?

You join Soket AI. This page and every offer letter use soket.ai only.

Where is the work?

Most roles are Bengaluru-first, at Indicube Miniforest, J.P. Nagar. Research roles can start remote with planned time on site.

How do I apply?

Open the role and submit through Northstarz. Recruiters write from @soket.ai or the ATS. We do not ask for payment or banking details to process an offer.

Can I apply to more than one role?

Pick the role whose workload you can already describe. If another fit is clearer, we will move you.

How long does hiring take?

A complete loop is usually two to four weeks after the work sample. We give written feedback when the loop was long enough to be useful.

What does compensation include?

Cash plus equity on full-time offers. Some technical roles already publish bands on the role page. The offer states cash, equity, start date, manager, and a 30-60-90 plan.

I need an interview adjustment.

Write to connect@soket.ai before the scheduled session. We will change format, time, or tooling.
